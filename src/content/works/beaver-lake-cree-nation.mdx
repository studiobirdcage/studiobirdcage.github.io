The Beaver Lake Cree Nation contracted Division [1] Media Corp to redesign their band's marketing/informative website and I was tasked with the project. The site was developed using a proprietary CMS called DevLive (Devlive was developed in house at Division [1]). With DevLive, I was able to leverage the open nature of the admin panel to develop a new dynamic events calendar and custom job posting board.

DevLive was also used for its wysiwyg editor, as the client wanted to have the ability to edit the content of their site without the need of a developer. With the DevLive editor it is easy for a client to add new menu items/groups, pages and content with little to no training in development.
