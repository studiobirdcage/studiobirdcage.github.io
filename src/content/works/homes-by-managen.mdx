Homes By Managen were in dire need of a new, modern marketing site for their company. Their old site was functional but was looking very dated. A major issues was that it was not mobile friendly nor did it really emphasize the amazing homes that they are known for. Since they were already familiar with WordPress, they wanted the new site to also used it as a CMS. With 30+ year in business, they already had a strong company wide style guide with a great logo, strong colors and particular fonts ready for me to use which made my job much easier.

The marketing site has some pretty basic functionality. I created a simple home slider and added a custom post type for adding new show homes/available homes. The site was developed to be easy to update once it was handed off so a lot of time was dedicated to making the WordPress back end easy to use. As for the design, The idea was to make the images the main focus and keep all other design elements very minimal.
