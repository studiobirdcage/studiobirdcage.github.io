Industrial Machine Inc. is one of the leading distributors of snow removal, road maintenance, landscaping, ice resurfacing and other industrial equipment in western Canada. With the redesign of their marketing/inventory site, IMI wanted something that was easy to use, showed off their every-growing line of products and of course looked amazing. They also needed a weekly email newsletter to be developed and a graphic designer to make everything look great. I was happy to be part of the team that was able to fill all of these roles for IMI. 

Exciting features that I developed for this project were a custom featured news slider, product quote request system, fully searchable product video archive and used equipment management software built into the proprietary CMS. I also worked closely with the client on all product/page copy writing, content population and graphic design elements.
